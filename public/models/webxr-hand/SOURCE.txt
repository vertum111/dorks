Game uses right.glb: a skinned hand with 25 joint nodes.
At runtime the flat tracked-joint skeleton is reparented into finger chains,
preserving its bind pose. Thumb and index are posed using inverse kinematics.
Thumb/index IK uses an analytic bend plane and a fixed distal approach direction.
Other fingers stay relaxed above the table through their real joints. Geometry is not procedural.
